# PriAI

> Transform Your Knowledge Into AI

---

# Vision

PriAI enables anyone to build a private, domain-specific AI from their own knowledge.

Instead of relying on a general-purpose AI trained on internet-scale data, PriAI transforms user-provided books, notes, documents, and study material into a specialized AI capable of providing accurate, source-grounded answers within its learned domain.

The resulting AI behaves like a subject expert—it studies the provided knowledge once and remains available to answer questions repeatedly, even without an internet connection.

> **PriAI doesn't try to know everything. It helps you build an AI that knows exactly what you want it to know.**

---

# The Problem

Modern AI assistants are designed to answer almost every question.

While this makes them extremely versatile, many users need something different: an AI that deeply understands a specific subject rather than knowing a little about everything.

Students, researchers, professionals, teachers, and organizations often spend hours searching through books, notes, manuals, PDFs, documentation, and research papers to find one specific answer.

Even after reading all the material, locating the right information later is slow and frustrating.

Users need an assistant that has already studied their knowledge and can retrieve it instantly.

---

# Our Solution

PriAI allows users to build their own AI by importing their existing knowledge.

Rather than training a custom language model, PriAI combines local language models with user-provided knowledge to create a specialized AI assistant.

After the knowledge compilation process is complete, the AI answers questions entirely from the uploaded material while remaining private and available offline.

# What PriAI Is

PriAI is a platform for building domain-specific AI assistants.

Every AI created with PriAI focuses on the knowledge selected by the user.

Supported knowledge domains can include:

- Computer Science
- Competitive Exam Preparation
- Research Papers
- Company Documentation
- Product Manuals
- Legal Documents
- Medical References
- Personal Notes
- Religious Texts
- Any stable knowledge domain

---

# What PriAI Is Not

PriAI is not a general-purpose AI assistant.

It is not designed to answer questions outside the knowledge provided by the user.

It is not intended for live internet search, breaking news, or rapidly changing information.

Instead, PriAI excels in stable knowledge domains where accuracy, privacy, explainability, and offline availability are more valuable than constantly updated information.

---

# Why PriAI

Unlike general-purpose AI assistants, PriAI answers questions using only the knowledge chosen by the user.

Unlike cloud-based AI services, PriAI is designed to work privately and offline after the knowledge has been compiled.

Unlike manually searching through documents, PriAI enables natural language conversations across an entire knowledge collection.

---

# Core Principles

## Knowledge Ownership

Users own their knowledge.

Users decide what their AI learns.

Users remain in control of their data.

---

## Privacy First

Knowledge remains on the user's device whenever possible.

No internet connection is required after the AI has been built.

---

## Domain Expertise

Every AI specializes in a single knowledge domain.

A focused scope improves relevance, consistency, and reduces hallucinations.

---

## Source-Grounded Responses

Responses should always be grounded in the imported knowledge.

Whenever possible, the AI should reference the source material instead of generating unsupported information.

---

## Honest Intelligence

If the answer cannot be derived from the imported knowledge, PriAI should clearly state that the question is outside its current knowledge scope.
