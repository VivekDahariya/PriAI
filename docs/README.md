# Why PriAI?

Most AI assistants try to answer **everything**.

PriAI does the opposite.

Instead of learning from the entire internet, PriAI allows users to build a specialized AI that learns only from the knowledge they provide.

The result is a private AI assistant that remains focused, explainable, and available without an internet connection.

---

# The Problem

Finding one answer inside hundreds of pages is slow.

Students search through textbooks.

Developers search documentation.

Researchers search papers.

Companies search internal manuals.

Even after reading everything once, locating the right information repeatedly wastes valuable time.

General-purpose AI models may answer beyond the provided material, while manual searching interrupts productivity.

---

# The Solution

PriAI transforms your knowledge into a personal AI assistant.

Upload your documents once.

PriAI processes and organizes the knowledge.

After that, simply ask questions in natural language.

Every response is generated using only your imported knowledge.
